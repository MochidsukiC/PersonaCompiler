APPENDIX · A5
Cost and scale: not everyone thinks every turn
TIER 0
Background
No individual inference. Moves by rules and statistics. Cheapest.
TIER 1
On events
Thinks only when something happens to it.
TIER 2
Every phase
Individual inference each morning, noon, evening, night.
TIER 3
Main cast
Every phase, highest effort. The characters your story depends on.
Model and effort per role
Parent, residents, and facilities each get their own model and reasoning effort. Tiers map to effort automatically.
Rate limits are survivable
Up to five retries with persisted backoff; the scheduler holds new inference while waiting. Quota errors stop cleanly.
What scales is the output
Thousands of concurrently thinking residents are out of scope today. The compiled package is static JSON: it costs nothing at runtime.
