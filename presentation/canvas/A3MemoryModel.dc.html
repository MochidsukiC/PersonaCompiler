APPENDIX · A3
Experienced, remembered, and recallable are three different things
1 · EXPERIENCED
Who could hear it
Every utterance records its recipients, computed from voice level, 3D distance, and house walls. Sleeping residents hear nothing, and it is never redelivered.
A memory may only cite what its owner actually received.
2 · REMEMBERED
What the resident keeps
At sleep the resident chooses 0 to 5 new memories from up to 100 candidates and keeps at most 100 in total. Forgetting is real: dropped memories stay only in the objective log.
3 · RECALLABLE NOW
What comes to mind
A seeded, decaying probability (importance × 2^(−Δturns/40)) plus semantic matching in a short-lived thread on the resident's own model. Reproducible per run; never up to us.
Relationships are text, not numbers.
A's view of B and B's view of A are separate objects, each citing the memories that formed it, each with revision history. No affection meters anywhere in the code.
