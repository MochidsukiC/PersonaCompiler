How it works
02 / 05
STEP 1
Describe a town
A few paragraphs. Setting, people, facilities.
STEP 2
A parent agent interviews you
Drafts the spec. You approve a revision, then it generates the first residents.
STEP 3
Residents live it
One Codex thread each. Work, talk, remember, sleep, marry, die.
STEP 4
Compile
When the town's story ends, every survivor is compiled from what they lived.
STEP 5
Game-ready package
Character, relationships, memories, schedule, runtime prompt. Every trait cites its evidence.
Nobody scripts who becomes friends. The harness only keeps the world honest.
